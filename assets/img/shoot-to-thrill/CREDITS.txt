CAPTURE SCOPE
Both PNG stills and both MP4 clips use an earlier handling checkpoint.
They do not depict the later unresolved walking/contact candidate.
The source recording passed its render guard with 1,201 contiguous unique
rendered markers numbered 1 through 1,201. The public viewing copies are:
- handling-sequence.mp4: first 1,200 source frames, 20 seconds at 60 FPS.
- recoil-sequence.mp4: source frames 481–720, 4 seconds at 60 FPS.
- prototype-ready.png: unchanged source frame 120.
- recoil-poster.png: unchanged source frame 620.
Video is encoded as H.264 at 1280 x 720, normal speed, without audio.
The original PNG sequence established the render checks; lossy viewing
copies are intended for visual review. Fixture telemetry uses its own
frame clock and is not asserted to align exactly to encoded video frames.

CHARTS AND DATA
Charts by Jean Brito with Codex assistance, generated from recorded
prototype measurements. measurements.json supplies public chart values,
units, and limitations. The walking candidate remains unaccepted.
These measurements are not firearm physics or reference-game parameters.
Output-size charts measure bytes, not model tokens, time, or billing.

CODE EXAMPLES
examples.py contains newly written standalone teaching examples, not
private game implementation. The examples are intentionally incomplete
as production validators; each article explains their scope.
